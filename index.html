    Structure: 2×90s + 4×60s + 4×30s + 4×15s hard, each with an equal-length float. One set = 20 minutes exactly, on top of your warm-up and cool-down.

    A float isn’t a jog — hold it between threshold and marathon pace, quicker than marathon effort, so you keep clearing lactate rather than fully recovering. Coach David Roche pegs float effort as easy-to-moderate; running coach Steve Magness puts experienced runners’ float around marathon pace as the slow end of that range.
